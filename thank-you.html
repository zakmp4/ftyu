While You Wait

Worth doing before we arrive

Read your water meter

Turn every tap and appliance off, photograph the meter, wait an hour and photograph it again. If the numbers moved, that's real evidence — for us and for an insurer.

Photograph the damage

Damp patches, bubbling paint, lifting boards, stained ceilings. Dated photos of how it's progressing are genuinely useful.

Dig out recent bills

A few quarters of water bills side by side make an unexplained jump obvious, and help us judge how long it's been running.
